# Community road safety

Shared language for Open Tallahassee's free road-safety resource.

## Language

**Donation**:
A voluntary contribution supporting data acquisition and necessary project hosting and maintenance, not a purchase of access, a map, or priority.
_Avoid_: Purchase, map fee, subscription

**City request**:
An expression of interest in a crash map for another city, with no payment requirement, donor priority, or guaranteed delivery date.
_Avoid_: Order, purchase request

**Data request**:
A request for access to project data through a handled request process, distinct from requesting a map for a city.
_Avoid_: Public download, city request, purchase

**Crash**:
A recorded traffic event, distinct from the individual people involved or harmed in it.

**Fatal crash**:
A crash in which at least one person was killed, as classified by the source data. One fatal crash can involve multiple fatalities.

**Fatality**:
A person killed in a crash, as classified by the source data. A fatality count measures people killed, not the number of fatal crashes; fatalities remain a core part of this website.

**Person injured**:
A person with an injury recorded in the source data. Injury counts measure people, separately from crash counts and fatalities, using documented source classifications.

**Data coverage**:
The geographic area and time periods represented by a dataset, including known gaps.
_Avoid_: Last updated

**Data refresh date**:
The date a published dataset was refreshed; it does not by itself establish when the crashes occurred.
_Avoid_: Coverage end date
