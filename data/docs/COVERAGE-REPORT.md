## Coverage window

## What this data cannot show yet

- **Fatalities (people killed).** The file flags whether a crash was fatal but doesn't count the people killed. The site shows fatal crashes only until person-level records are reconciled.
- **Injury severity.** There are no injury fields, so the site offers Fatal and Nonfatal, not Injury. "Nonfatal" includes crashes with or without injuries.
- **Completeness.** Whether every quarter is complete hasn't been checked against FLHSMV totals. 2020 Q2 is noticeably lower, which is consistent with reduced travel in spring 2020 but hasn't been confirmed.
- **City limits over time.** The 2021 city boundary is applied to all crashes.
